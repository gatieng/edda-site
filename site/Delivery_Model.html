Delivery Model

Three engagement models, one consistent quality of delivery. We adapt to your context, not the other way around.

Flexible engagement, consistent delivery.

  • We propose three different engagement models so you can pick the one that fits your context, your team and your governance.
  • Whatever the model, we keep the same standards: senior people, transparent reporting and a clear definition of done.
  • Our teams operate from Luxembourg and Brussels, close to our clients and aligned with their operating constraints.

Engagement models, chosen to fit the mission.

From a short advisory sprint to a multi-year managed service centre. We pick the engagement shape that fits the work, not the other way round.

Advise

Consulting

Senior consultants embedded in your team to frame the problem, architect the solution and steer delivery.

  • Architecture & technology strategy
  • Audits & roadmaps
  • Programme & project governance
Deliver

Project delivery

Fixed-scope, fixed-outcome engagements. We own the plan, the quality gates and the go-live.

  • Data & AI implementations
  • Accessibility remediation programmes
Industrialise

  • Coaching, cookbooks, continuous improvement
