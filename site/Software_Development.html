Software Development

Technology is the heart of our business, but never our starting point in the design of customised solutions.

Our approachCustom by default, never off-the-shelf.

No two clients are the same. We always begin by asking our analysts to research both the environment in which you operate and your specific business, and our UX experts to focus on the user experience requirements. We also address your individual needs as regards to security (Security Development Lifecycle). Only then can we start to develop a bespoke solution for you.

At a second stage, our technology experts define the applicative architecture and the type of technology needed: cloud, on-premise or hybrid.

You can rely on both our proven methodology and our experience in running multiple fixed-price or Agile projects. Our development process adheres at all times to ALM (Application Lifecycle Management) standards and best practices.

It's not just about technology.

Every engagement starts the same way: we listen, research and design before we build. Technology is the answer, never the question.

  1. 01

    Business analysis

    Our analysts study your environment, business model and operating constraints before a single line of code.

  2. 02

    UX & user research

    UX experts focus on the people who will actually use the product: real workflows, real friction, real adoption.

  3. 03

    Security by design

    Security Development Lifecycle baked in from day one. No bolt-ons, no afterthoughts.

  4. 04

    Architecture & tech

    Only now do we define the applicative architecture and target infrastructure: cloud, on-prem or hybrid.

  5. 05

    Build & deliver

    Fixed-price or Agile, your call. Proven methodology and ALM standards keep every release production-ready.

Four ways we engage on software.

From greenfield builds to long-term application care: pick the engagement shape that fits the work.

01

Application Development

Customised software development: the commissioning, development and release of a software product tailored to a single entity. We help organisations focus on their core business issues, reduce costs, improve IT operational efficiency, accelerate time-to-market and free existing staff to focus on mission-critical initiatives.

02

Application Maintenance & Evolution

We can take on the existing scope of a particular application and work on its further evolution. If you want to bring your company completely up to date in terms of technology, and call on a service provider who goes beyond the extra mile, this is for you. We also offer customised UX services to make existing applications more useful, more user-friendly and more competitive.

03

Architecture & Advisory

We help in many different areas: information system mapping, application architectures, development methods, integration of business software applications, implementation of a particular SOA, and analysis of the impact of a migration to cloud computing.

04

Project Management

Three different ways to run your project, according to your needs: consulting, project-based delivery, or via our EDDA Factory. With the project and Factory options, we take on the full management for you.
