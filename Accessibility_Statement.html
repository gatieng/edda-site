Accessibility Statement.

EDDA is committed to making its website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities, in line with the EU Web Accessibility Directive (2016/2102) and the European Accessibility Act.

Accessibility is part of how we design and build digital products — for our clients and for ourselves. This statement explains where this website stands today and how to reach us if something gets in your way.

Our commitment

EDDA wants as many people as possible to be able to use this website. We aim to meet recognised accessibility standards so that, for example, you can:

  • navigate most of the site using a keyboard alone;
  • use the site with a screen reader and other assistive technologies;
  • resize text up to 200% without loss of content or functionality;
  • read content with sufficient colour contrast and clear focus indicators.

Accessibility is also one of our service areas, so we hold our own properties to the same standard we recommend to clients.

Conformance status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) define requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities, at three levels: A, AA and AAA.

This website is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 level AA and the harmonised European standard EN 301 549. “Partially conformant” means that some parts of the content do not yet fully meet the standard; the known issues are listed below and are being addressed.

Measures we take

To keep accessibility front of mind, EDDA:

  • includes accessibility in the definition of done for new features;
  • uses semantic HTML, landmarks and ARIA only where native semantics fall short;
  • tests with keyboard navigation and screen readers during development;
  • runs automated checks and periodic manual audits;
  • provides skip links, visible focus styles and respects reduced-motion preferences.

Compatibility with browsers and assistive technology

This website is designed to be compatible with recent versions of the following, on desktop and mobile:

  • browsers: Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari;
  • screen readers: NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver;
  • operating-system zoom and browser text resizing.

The site may not display optimally in browsers older than three major versions, or with JavaScript disabled.

Known limitations

Despite our efforts, some limitations remain. We are working to resolve the following:

  • a small number of decorative animations rely on motion (these are disabled when “reduce motion” is set);
  • some third-party embedded content may not fully meet our accessibility targets;
  • a few legacy PDF documents are being remediated or replaced with accessible HTML.

Tell us — we want to fix it.

If you cannot access part of this website or need information in an alternative format, contact us and we will help and aim to provide a response within five working days.

Feedback & contact

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of this website. Please let us know if you encounter barriers:

  • email: accessibility@edda.lu
  • phone: +352 28 86 07
  • or use our contact form.

When reporting an issue, it helps to include the page address, what you were trying to do, and the browser or assistive technology you were using.

Enforcement procedure

If you contact us with a complaint and you are not satisfied with our response, you can refer the matter to the relevant national supervisory body. In Luxembourg, the monitoring of web accessibility is handled by the designated public authority responsible for the Web Accessibility Directive.
