From experiments to operated AI products.

Six engagement shapes, picked to fit where you are on the AI maturity curve.

01 / Strategy

AI use-case discovery & framing

Short, structured engagements to identify high-value, low-risk AI opportunities, scored on feasibility, value and regulatory exposure.

02 / Build

Copilots & agentic workflows

Domain-grounded LLM assistants and multi-step agents. RAG, tools, evaluation harnesses and the orchestration to keep them sane in production.

03 / Build

Custom ML & predictive models

Forecasting, scoring, anomaly detection. Trained on your data, deployed as APIs or batch jobs, monitored for drift and decay.

04 / Integrate

GenAI in existing platforms

Embedding LLM features into your CRM, ECM or business apps, with the security, identity and audit your platform team expects.

05 / Operate

Evaluation & MLOps

Continuous evaluation pipelines, prompt registries, model monitoring and retraining loops. AI you can actually run, not just demo.

06 / Govern

AI governance & risk

Inventory, risk classification, bias and explainability assessments, and the documentation needed for the EU AI Act and your auditors.
